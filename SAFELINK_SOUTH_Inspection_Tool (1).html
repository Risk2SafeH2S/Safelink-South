Legal basis. The Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 (Act 37 of 2020) came into force on 21 November 2025 (S.O. 5321(E)), repealing the Factories Act 1948, Mines Act 1952, CLRA 1970, ISMW Act 1979, Dock Workers (SHW) Act 1986, BOCW Act 1996 and seven other enactments under S.143(1). Final Central Rules were notified on 8 May 2026. State rules under the OSH Code are at different stages across the six states; under S.143(3) the machinery of the repealed Acts and the existing State Factories Rules continues to operate so far as it is not contrary to the Code, until State OSH Rules replace it.

Verify before citing. State rule status, fire NOC categories and validity periods change frequently. Confirm each state citation against the current gazette and the relevant DISH / State Fire Service circular before issuing a report to a client or a regulator. This instrument records professional judgement; it is not a legal opinion.
